# How Mainstream News Media Integrate Prediction-Market Odds (2024–2026)

## Partnerships: which outlets show Kalshi/Polymarket odds, how, and since when

### Takeaway
Between mid-2025 and fall 2026, almost every major US outlet signed a commercial deal with Kalshi or Polymarket. Kalshi took TV news: CNN (Dec 2025), CNBC (Dec 2025), Fox Corp (Apr 2026) and CBS (Sep 2026). AP licenses data to Kalshi (Mar 2026). Polymarket took print, digital and social: X/xAI (Jun 2025), Yahoo Finance (Nov 2025, later wound down), Dow Jones/WSJ (Jan 2026) and Substack (2024, expanded Feb 2026). Google Finance/Search shows both (Nov 2025). Odds usually appear as branded on-air tickers, homepage or article widgets, and embeds. Most deals are paid, and their terms were not disclosed.

### Cited Findings

- Announced on December 2, 2025 (a Tuesday); Kalshi's post is dated December 3, 2025.
- Kalshi data is used across CNN programming, "led by CNN Chief Data Analyst Harry Enten." The newsroom, data and production teams can use the data for storylines and visuals.
- A "Kalshi-powered real-time news ticker" runs during segments that feature Kalshi data.

**CNN on-air use and terms**

- Source: [TheWrap, Jan 16 2026](https://www.thewrap.com/media-platforms/journalism/polymarket-kalshi-cnn-cnbc-dow-jones/)

**CNN: what it says it gets from the deal**
- Kalshi data appears in a segment called "The Odds."
- Per a source, CNN has no equity in Kalshi and earns nothing from customer acquisition, trades or contracts.
- Its editorial segments carry no affiliate links.
- CNN editorial employees are prohibited from trading on prediction markets.
- Source: [Bloomberg via Tucson.com, Nov 2 2026](https://tucson.com/news/nation-world/business/article_1500cb9f-37da-48fd-a340-e0f6706d498b.html)

**CNBC–Kalshi**

- It also covers digital and subscription products.
- Kalshi hosts a CNBC-branded page of markets that CNBC editors select.
- Sources: [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/tvnewser/ticker-cnn-and-cnbc-strike-partnership-with-kalshi/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/cnbc-kalshi-prediction-deal-ms-now-versant/)

**CBS News–Kalshi**

- Real-time contract prices appear alongside CBS polling, including the Battleground Tracker.
- Unveiled on CBS Mornings by Anthony Salvanto.
- Focus: congressional control, plus Texas and Maine.

**AP–Kalshi (data flows the other way)**

- Kalshi's Jaron Zhou: it "enhances Kalshi's election night experience."

### Inferences
- The pattern is that markets pay for distribution and credibility. TV deals put odds on air as branded tickers and segments. Print and digital deals use widgets and embeds.
- Elections are the carve-out: the networks with the strongest decision desks (CNN, Fox) keep market odds out of election-night calling.

### Gaps
- No financial terms were found for any deal.
- No primary sources found on Robinhood, Coinbase, DraftKings or FanDuel prediction products as media integrations. That was outside what these searches surfaced.
- No primary confirmation that the Polymarket–X/xAI deal is still active in 2026.
- No PredictIt media deals found.

## 2024 general election night: how markets and outlets interacted

### Takeaway
In 2024 no major TV network had a formal market partnership. Coverage of markets ran mostly in crypto and finance outlets. The story afterward was that Polymarket and Kalshi priced a Trump win hours before the AP called the race (AP's call came at 5:34 a.m. ET on Nov 6). That "vindication" narrative helped drive the 2025–26 wave of media deals.

### Cited Findings
- Polymarket's Trump odds crossed 95% at about 11:43 p.m. ET, nearly six hours before AP's 5:34 a.m. ET call (Wisconsin). — [Economist Writing Every Day blog](https://economistwritingeveryday.com/2024/11/06/big-win-for-prediction-markets/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2024-11-06-donald-trump-polymarket-324738)
- Trump went into Election Day as roughly a 60/40 market favorite. — [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/41751501)
- Kalshi and Polymarket topped Apple's App Store on election night. Polymarket said it conveyed a Trump victory "two to three hours ahead of traditional media." — [Business Insider via AOL](https://www.aol.com/polymarket-predicted-trumps-win-now-092738615.html)
- Kalshi had more than 500,000 concurrent users and $476 million wagered on the presidential outcome. Kalshi's Trump odds went from 57% to 90% during the day. — [Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2024/11/06/us-election-results-donald-trumps-odds-surge-to-90-on-betting-markets/)
- CoinDesk: the returns were "a shocker if you only watched CNN. But not if you'd looked at the betting all along." — [CoinDesk](https://hotfix.coindesk.com/business/2024/11/06/polymarket-prediction-betting-markets-vindicated-by-trumps-strong-showing)
- Substack's Polymarket embeds were already live in 2024 newsletters. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/242455/polymarket-embeds-on-substack)

### Inferences
- "Markets beat the networks" in 2024 compares market prices with formal race calls. It isn't like-for-like: decision desks need statistical certainty, while markets price probability.

### Gaps
- No primary source found for specific anchors citing markets on air on 2024 election night.
- A claim that Fox called the 2024 race at 1:46 a.m. ET appeared only in an unverified user comment, so it is excluded.

## 2026 midterms (Nov 3, 2026) election night

### Takeaway
Going into the night, markets had Democrats as heavy favorites. Eve-of-election figures ranged from about 93–98% for the House and 75–90% for the Senate, depending on source and time. **I found no verified source for "98%+ for both chambers before polls closed."** Partner networks CNN, Fox and CBS said they would keep odds out of election-night calls. Kalshi ran its own live "99% KALSHI PROJECTION" feed, which amounts to a market-based race-calling product. Semafor's day-after verdict: markets "correctly called the Democratic sweep" but missed Texas governor (Abbott's loss) and Democratic wins in Iowa and South Carolina Senate races.

### Cited Findings
**Eve-of-election odds**
- Kalshi: Democrats 93% House, 75% Senate. Polymarket: 94.5% and 75.5%. — [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/midterm-elections-odds-prediction-spending-house-and-senate-1236938339/)
- About $1.9 billion was wagered on US races, Kalshi the larger share. — [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/midterm-elections-odds-prediction-spending-house-and-senate-1236938339/)
- One affiliate tracker put Democrats near 98% House and 90% Senate on November 3. Treat that as promotional. — [Oddschecker](https://www.oddschecker.com/us/insight/specials/politics/20261103-2026-midterm-election-tracker-live-odds-and-prediction-market-prices-for-2026-election-night)
- Markets favored Democrats in all 7 top swing Senate races less than 24 hours before voting. — [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/politics/prediction-markets-now-favor-democrats-to-win-all-7-of-the-top-swing-senate-races/)

**Network election-night policies**
- CNN: odds would not appear on the Magic Wall or be referenced on air election night. A spokesperson cited "strict editorial guidelines."
- Fox: calls races with its own polling unit.
- CBS: no prediction odds in newscasts.
- Bloomberg's analysis of Dune data put election-related trading on the two platforms at about $1.5 billion.
- Source: [Bloomberg via Tucson.com, Nov 2 2026](https://tucson.com/news/nation-world/business/article_1500cb9f-37da-48fd-a340-e0f6706d498b.html)

**Kalshi's live "projections" page**
- Entries labeled "99% KALSHI PROJECTION," running from 5:45 p.m. ET to 2:34 a.m. ET.
- Disclaimer: projections "do not imply market resolution or that races have been called."
- Also posted "NEW ALL-TIME HIGH" and "JUST IN" market-move alerts and poll-closing alerts.
- House control: Democrats 97.2% at 6:55 p.m. ET. No Senate control figure.
- No comparison with AP or network calls.
- Source: [Kalshi News live page](https://news.kalshi.com/p/2026-midterm-election-night-live-projected-winners)

**Texas governor: markets ahead of the call**
- Kalshi posted at 8:06 p.m. ET that Greg Abbott was "no longer favored." — [Kalshi live page](https://news.kalshi.com/p/2026-midterm-election-night-live-projected-winners)
- AP projected Hinojosa (D) the winner, about 52.5% to 46.0%, as of 3:22 a.m. ET on Nov 4. — [MultiState](https://www.multistate.us/elections/governor-race/texas-2026)

**CNN live blog**
- A search-result summary says CNN's live coverage noted traders bet $2 million on Kalshi that Abbott would win, a couple of hours before he was projected to lose. I could not fetch it directly (HTTP 451), so this is unconfirmed. — [CNN live](https://cnn.com/2026/11/03/politics/live-news/midterm-election-congress)

**Semafor's day-after analysis**

### Inferences
- In 2026 the "markets call races" function moved onto the platforms themselves (Kalshi's projection feed, AP data on Kalshi). Partner networks kept results and odds separate on air.
- The Texas case is the clearest example of markets moving well before a formal call.

## Live "broadcast-style" market displays

### Takeaway
The closest things to broadcast-style market displays are CNN's and CNBC's on-air Kalshi tickers and Kalshi's own live projections feed, which mimics a network decision desk. Dow Jones homepage widgets and auto-refreshing Substack and Google embeds are the digital equivalents. I found no network that ran a full live odds board on election night 2026.

## Journalistic standards debates

### Takeaway
The criticism is sustained. The main arguments: markets aren't polls, conflicts of interest from paid deals, insider-trading risk, and gambling promotion. Outlets responded with disclosures, staff trading bans and election-night carve-outs, not by dropping the deals.

### Cited Findings
**CJR Tow Center ("A bet is not a poll," C.J. Robinson, May 7, 2026)**
- Traders aren't a representative sample, and market moves can't be audited.
- Sports were about 90% of Kalshi's 2025 volume.
- G. Elliott Morris: "uncritical wholesale acceptance of their numbers ... very bad."
- Nathaniel Rakich: "There are responsible and irresponsible ways to cover it."
- Source: [CJR](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/a-bet-is-not-a-poll-polymarket-kalshi-journalists-wary-predict-future.php)

**Paid placement and insider trading (TheWrap, Jan 16 2026)**
- Sen. Chris Murphy: "CNN has a lucrative partnership with Kalshi, but it's wildly corrupt."
- Rep. Ritchie Torres said the Maduro-capture bet bore "the hallmarks of insider trading."
- CNN: the data "has no impact on editorial judgment."
- Dow Jones's Nolly Evans compared the issue with insider trading in stocks.
- Source: [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/media-platforms/journalism/polymarket-kalshi-cnn-cnbc-dow-jones/)

**Media watchdogs**
- Progressive watchdogs condemned CNN's deal. FAIR's Jim Naureckas called markets "highly susceptible to gaming." — [Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/media/progressive-media-watchdogs-bash-cnns-kalshi-partnership/)

### Inferences
- Standard practice by 2026 has three parts: disclose the commercial relationship, ban staff trading, and keep market odds out of race calls. Disclosure language varies; CNBC's is the most explicit.
