# Dedicated prediction-market news sites, aggregators and dashboards

Research done 2026-11-04. Many sources here are third-party directories (pm.wiki, polyzone.pages.dev, GitHub "awesome" lists) or vendor self-descriptions. These are flagged where relevant. Audience numbers are rarely public.

## Which dedicated sites exist (news, newsletters, editorial products)?

### Takeaway
The market operators run the most prominent "markets-as-news" outlets themselves: Polymarket's The Oracle Substack and Kalshi's news.kalshi.com. Around them is a small group of independent outlets: Election Betting Odds, Prediction News, Eventual, Boring News (Not Boring), Adjacent News, Sentinel, the Forecasting Newsletter and Next Event Horizon. Distribution has grown fastest through mainstream media and platform deals: CNN, CNBC and Fox with Kalshi; Dow Jones and Substack with Polymarket; and Google Finance with both.

### Cited Findings

**Market-affiliated (owned by an exchange)**
- **The Oracle by Polymarket** (Substack). Launched July 2024 alongside Polymarket's Substack embed partnership. It is Polymarket's "first news product": roundups of notable markets plus in-depth analyses — [MPost](https://mpost.io/polymarket-and-substack-partner-to-launch-prediction-market-embeds-and-debut-first-news-product-the-oracle); [Substack announcement](https://on.substack.com/p/dynamic-prediction-market-tables); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/242455/polymarket-embeds-on-substack)
  - A third-party directory lists it as twice weekly, with authors including Neil Paine (ex-FiveThirtyEight) — [Reletter](https://reletter.com/publications/polymarkets-substack)
  - One aggregator claims it has more than 70K subscribers. That figure is unverified — [newsletterly](https://newsletterly.io/newsletter/the-oracle-by-polymarket-substack)
  - Polymarket announced an exclusive Substack partnership in 2026 — [Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/polymarket-brings-live-probability-tables-to-substack-authors/)
- **Polymarket 2026 Midterms Hub.** Launched Sept 16, 2026. It has live markets for Senate, House, Governor and local races plus an interactive electoral map, with more than 560 midterm markets. This is a dashboard, not a newsroom — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/polymarket-launches-2026-midterms-hub-302880619.html)
- **Polymarket Institute.** A 2026 research initiative on prediction markets — [PR Newswire Polymarket feed](https://www.prnewswire.com/news/polymarket/)
- **Polymarket Live Ticker** (ticker.polymarket.com). An official widget that streams live market data for streams and websites — [Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- **Kalshi News** (news.kalshi.com). Kalshi's own editorial blog and newsroom with named writers.
  - Its author page shows contributors who cover politics, entertainment and sports, including a former Decrypt reporter and guest writers — [Kalshi News authors](https://news.kalshi.com/authors)
  - It also hosts press releases — [Kalshi press](https://news.kalshi.com/press)
- **Kalshi Research.** Announced Dec 22, 2025, as an internal unit that gives academics data access.
  - Its debut study claimed Kalshi's CPI forecasts beat the Wall Street consensus. Secondary coverage cites "40%" outperformance, but I did not verify the methodology — [Kalshi News](https://news.kalshi.com/p/kalshi-launches-research-arm-prediction-markets); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/prediction-market-research-kalshi-performance)
- **Kalshi media partnerships.**
  - Kalshi is CNN's official prediction market partner. Kalshi data runs across CNN programming, led by Chief Data Analyst Harry Enten — [Kalshi News](https://news.kalshi.com/p/kalshi-cnn-prediction-market-partnership)
  - Kalshi also has CNBC and Fox News deals — [Wikipedia: Kalshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalshi)
  - CNBC discloses a commercial relationship with Kalshi, including a minority investment — [CNBC 2026-11-02](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/prediction-market-traders-now-see-nearly-75percent-chance-of-democrats-winning-the-senate.html)
  - Critics say that since April, CNBC has had a dedicated reporter writing Kalshi-market articles, without always disclosing the relationship — [Public Notice](https://www.publicnotice.co/p/kalshi-cnn-cnbc)
  - Puck covers what Kalshi gets from sponsoring news orgs (Nov 2026) — [Puck](https://puck.news/media/2026/11/what-kalshi-gets-from-sponsoring-news-orgs)
- **Polymarket and Dow Jones.** Polymarket has a contract with Dow Jones — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/features/2026-prediction-markets-polymarket-kalshi/)

**Independent (or partner) editorial products**
- **Election Betting Odds** (electionbettingodds.com). Run by Maxim Lott and John Stossel.
  - It aggregates Betfair, Smarkets, PredictIt, Polymarket and Kalshi into one probability per race. It covers House and Senate control, a governor map, a Senate seat estimate and 2028 nominations — [electionbettingodds.com](https://electionbettingodds.com/)
  - It was live on Nov 4, 2026, with a header stamp of 2:24 AM EST. At that time it showed Senate control at Dem 99.6% with about $148.8M bet — [electionbettingodds.com](https://electionbettingodds.com/)
  - The footer still reads "2022 FTX Trading Ltd," a leftover from an old sponsorship — [electionbettingodds.com](https://electionbettingodds.com/)
  - Format: mostly a raw-numbers dashboard with light commentary.
- **Prediction News** (predictionnews.com). "The Leading Source for Prediction Markets News"
  - It covers the industry (deals, funding, launches) and also market-odds stories, for example "Kalshi midterm odds favor Democrats in four of five Trump-won governor races" — [Prediction News](https://predictionnews.com/); [story](https://predictionnews.com/story/kalshi-midterm-odds-favor-democrats-in-four-of-five-trump-won-governor-races)
  - Many stories are rewrites credited to other outlets. Pages promote PredictionEdge.com, an odds/polling tracker — [Jefferies story](https://predictionnews.com/story/jefferies-prediction-market-volume-jumps-55-to-new-record-in-october)
  - X handle: @PredictionNews_ — [X](https://x.com/PredictionNews_)
  - Owner and launch date not found.
- **Eventual.** A San Francisco news outlet staffed by working prediction-market traders rather than career reporters.
  - It has published daily on Substack since early July (2026 implied), plus a weekly or twice-weekly show, and has a data partnership with Polymarket — [Sovereign Magazine](https://www.sovereignmagazine.com/article/prediction-market-traders-newsroom-eventual)
  - Founders and funding: not in the excerpt (paywalled).
- **Boring News** (from Packy McCormick's Not Boring). Launched Dec 2024.
  - A daily weekday show picks 3 Polymarket stories. Perplexity and Grok supply context, ChatGPT writes the script, and an AI clone of Packy's voice reads it. Distribution is X, YouTube and podcasts, and each story links to the Polymarket contract — [Marginal Revolution](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2024/12/info-finance-via-boring-news.html); [Not Boring post mirror](https://fast-feed.onrender.com/productivity/notboring/12); [polyzone](https://polyzone.pages.dev/boring-news/)
  - Parent newsletter: about 200K–260K subscribers (sources conflict) — [getcoai](https://getcoai.com/news/founder-of-not-boring-shares-how-he-uses-ai-to-enhance-his-writing/)
  - Status in 2026: unconfirmed.
- **Adjacent News** (adj.news). Founder Lucas Kohorst. "Forward-looking news driven by prediction markets and forecasting platforms": articles plus a news/markets API — [LessWrong launch post](https://lesswrong.com/posts/aBgWfJbbGFctpnkEZ/launching-adjacent-news); [EA Forum](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/vqko6DCH5gBc3cexB/adjacent-news-articles-driven-by-forecasting-platforms)
  - It was bootstrapped, with a Manifund grant application — [Manifund](https://manifund.org/projects/adjacent-news)
  - It evolved into "Adjacent," a provider of event-contract and prediction-market indices. Adjacent raised a $2.5M pre-seed from VanEck, DCG, Maven11, Night Capital and UFO Holdings. Sources date the round to Jun 29 or July 2026 — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/adjacent_markets); [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/adjacentresearch); [TheSaaSNews](https://thesaasnews.com/news/adjacent-raises-2-5m-pre-seed)
  - So the original media product has pivoted toward indices and infrastructure.
- **Sentinel / Global Risks Weekly Roundup.** By Nuño Sempere (head of foresight), Tolga Bilge, Vidur Kapur and belikewater.
  - A weekly roundup of global catastrophic risks with forecasts, plus the "Sentinel Minutes" podcast. It is active through 2026 (issues #2–#8/2026 seen) — [Pocket Casts](https://pocketcasts.com/podcast/sentinel-minutes/21c31c70-f9e8-013d-471c-02bb5ef11301/us-buildup-in-middle-east-continues-russian-vessel-en-route-to-cuba-ai-time-horizons-lengthen-global-risks-weekly-roundup-82026-by-nuño-sempere-vidur-kapur-belikewater-tolga-bilge/348a2bf0-b43f-4dca-b943-8482bccd6abb)
  - It was founded in 2024 and uses AI filtering of news plus elite forecasters — [Longterm Wiki](https://www.longtermwiki.com/organizations/sentinel)
  - It uses its own forecasters, not only market prices.
- **Forecasting Newsletter** (forecasting.substack.com, Nuño Sempere). A monthly newsletter, about 7.9K subscribers per the Substack profile — [Substack](https://substack.com/@forecasting)
- **Next Event Horizon** (Substack). Industry analysis, for example "Top 10 Stories of Prediction Markets in 2025" and "Five Thoughts On Prediction Markets As Midterm Elections Arrive" — [Substack](https://nexteventhorizon.substack.com/p/the-top-10-stories-of-prediction-markets-2025); [midterms post](https://nexteventhorizon.substack.com/p/five-thoughts-on-election-markets)
- **PROPHET** (prophetnotes.com). A newsletter of data-driven forecasts on politics, finance and geopolitics — [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- **DeepNewz** (deepnewz.com).
  - Directories describe it as an AI news app that pairs stories with real-time prediction market odds and generates articles from raw data. It is Miami-based — [pm.wiki](https://pm.wiki/uk/projects/deepnewz); [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
  - Another review says it has no direct betting integration, only daily market recaps — [polyzone](https://polyzone.pages.dev/deepnewz/)
  - Conflicting and low-authority sources.
- **Pizzint Watch** (pizzint.watch). An OSINT dashboard (the "Pentagon pizza index") paired with Polymarket signals — [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- **Stocktwits.** Shows prediction market probabilities alongside trader discussion — [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)

**Platform integrations (not dedicated sites, but major distribution)**
- **Google Finance.** Announced Nov 6, 2025. It shows Kalshi and Polymarket odds when users ask about future events, rolling out first to Labs users — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-06/google-to-offer-kalshi-and-polymarket-data-on-finance-searches); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/consumer/google-kalshi-polymarket-prediction-markets-rcna242670)
- **Substack embeds.** Live Polymarket probability tables for any Substack author, since July 2024 — [Substack](https://on.substack.com/p/dynamic-prediction-market-tables)
- **Polymarket and Perplexity.** Partnership (Aug 2024) for news summaries on Polymarket market pages — [OODA Loop](https://oodaloop.com/briefs/2024/08/12/prediction-marketplace-polymarket-partners-with-perplexity-to-show-news-summaries)

**Defunct or changed**
- **Metaforecast** (metaforecast.org, a QURI multi-platform forecast search engine) now 308-redirects to quantifieduncertainty.org, so it appears discontinued or folded in — [metaforecast.org redirect, fetched 2026-11-04](https://metaforecast.org/)
- **Adjacent News** media product pivoted to Adjacent (indices); see above.

### Inferences
- The most-read "markets say" journalism in 2026 is probably not on dedicated sites at all. It is on CNN, CNBC and Google Finance through paid or commercial deals, which raises independence questions (Public Notice, Puck).
- The best-known editorial products are owned by the exchanges (The Oracle, Kalshi News), so they are not independent.
- Election Betting Odds remains the longest-running independent multi-market aggregator (since 2015/16, from memory, not verified here) and was live on midterm night.

### Gaps
- No verified audience numbers for Kalshi News, Prediction News, Eventual or Election Betting Odds.
- Owner and launch date of Prediction News not found.
- Eventual's founders and funding are paywalled.
- Whether Boring News is still producing in 2026 is not confirmed.
- I did not check Oddschecker politics, the Manifold or Metaculus news pages, or "The Prediction Market" newsletter (ran out of tool budget).

## Which aggregators combine multiple markets into one view?

### Takeaway
There are many 2025–26 aggregators, mostly trader-focused (arbitrage and price comparison) rather than editorial. Only a few include PredictIt. Election Betting Odds is the main consumer-facing, politics-first multi-market aggregator.

### Cited Findings
- **Election Betting Odds.** Betfair, Smarkets, PredictIt, Polymarket and Kalshi — [site](https://electionbettingodds.com/)
- **PredictionMarkets.US.** Kalshi, Polymarket and PredictIt side by side, with prices, fees and liquidity — [site](https://predictionmarkets.us/)
- **Prediktion.** Real-time odds from Polymarket, Kalshi and PredictIt, with alerts — [site](https://prediktion.market/)
- **Prediction Hunt.** Kalshi, Polymarket and PredictIt, with arbitrage detection and about 5-minute refresh — [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- **Predxy.** Merges matched questions across about 15 venues into one volume-weighted probability — [site](https://predxy.com/)
- **PredictMarketCap.** Polymarket, Kalshi, Limitless and Predict.fun, with rankings and arbitrage — [site](https://predictmarketcap.com/)
- **AggOdds.** Polymarket, Kalshi and Opinion — [site](https://aggodds.online/)
- **PredictionBell.** A Polymarket and Kalshi tracker — [site](https://predictionbell.com/)
- **Verso** (verso.trading). A "Bloomberg Terminal for prediction markets" (Kalshi and Polymarket) with a "Smart News Engine" and alerts. Founded 2024, said to be YC-backed (unverified, pm.wiki only) — [pm.wiki](https://pm.wiki/hi/compare/axiom-vs-verso); [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- **Trader terminals and aggregators:**
  - Kairos merges Kalshi, Polymarket and Predict.fun order books.
  - Matchr searches 1,500+ markets.
  - Synthesis does cross-market price comparison.
  - Hashdive does Polymarket and Kalshi analytics.
  - Markium does market aggregation.
  - Source for all four: [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- **Data APIs that unify venues:**
  - PolyRouter covers Kalshi, Polymarket and Limitless.
  - Dome offers unified APIs.
  - Predictefy covers 15+ venues.
  - PMXT is open source.
  - Apify has a scraper covering Kalshi, Polymarket, Manifold and PredictIt — [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools); [Apify](https://apify.com/tacps126/prediction-markets)
- **Single-venue analytics dashboards:**
  - Polymarket Analytics: [polymarketanalytics.com](https://polymarketanalytics.com/)
  - Dune dashboards (sealaunch trending topics; Dunedata cross-platform volume and open interest).
  - Blockworks and Artemis prediction-market sector analytics.
  - Source for the Dune, Blockworks and Artemis items: [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- **pm.wiki.** A directory and comparison of 350+ prediction-market projects — [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- A tools roundup for 2026 also exists — [bonus.com](https://www.bonus.com/prediction-markets/tools/)

### Inferences
- Most aggregators are built for arbitrage, so they present raw numbers with little or no narrative. Election Betting Odds and PredictionEdge are the politics-reader exceptions.
- The aggregator listings come from vendor sites and community lists. Traction, ownership and current status for most are unverified.

### Gaps
- No traffic data for any of these aggregators.
- Launch dates are unknown for most of them.

## Which use AI to auto-write news from market moves?

### Takeaway
A few products auto-generate news from odds: Boring News (fully AI-scripted and AI-voiced), DeepNewz (AI articles tied to odds, per directories), and Polysights (AI summaries). Verso advertises a "Smart News Engine." I found no well-funded, mainstream "AI newsroom from market moves" startup with press coverage.

### Cited Findings
- **Boring News:** odds from Polymarket, context from Perplexity and Grok, script from ChatGPT, and an AI voice clone. Marginal Revolution called it "All automated" — [Marginal Revolution](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2024/12/info-finance-via-boring-news.html)
- **DeepNewz:** "generates articles from raw data" paired with real-time odds. Directory claim only — [pm.wiki](https://pm.wiki/uk/projects/deepnewz)
- **Polysights:** AI analytics with summaries and alerts for Polymarket — [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- **Polysimplr:** a Polymarket interface with AI chat and analysis — [Awesome list](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)
- **Sentinel:** AI-filters millions of news items each week, then forecasters assess them — [Longterm Wiki](https://www.longtermwiki.com/organizations/sentinel)
- **Polymarket and Perplexity:** AI-generated news summaries on Polymarket market pages — [OODA Loop](https://oodaloop.com/briefs/2024/08/12/prediction-marketplace-polymarket-partners-with-perplexity-to-show-news-summaries)
- An extended search for "AI news generated from prediction market moves startup" surfaced none — [search noted; e.g. NPR on Meta's AI prediction-market app, which is a market, not news](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/24/nx-s1-5869486/meta-prediction-market-app-ai)

### Inferences
- AI-written "market moved, here's why" blurbs appear to be a feature built into exchange pages (Polymarket and Perplexity) and terminals more than a standalone news business.

### Gaps
- No confirmed evidence of whether Kalshi News uses AI-generated articles.
- TipRanks' "auto-generated newsdesk" covers Polymarket press releases, but that is not market-move-driven — [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/private-companies/polymarket-deepens-u-s-election-focus-with-2026-midterms-hub-launch)

## What launched in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
The 2025–26 boom brought exchange-run research and news arms, mainstream media and Google deals, Polymarket's midterms hub, trader-staffed outlets like Eventual, and dozens of aggregator and terminal startups. Kalshi and Polymarket together passed $44B of volume in 2025.

- **October 2026 record:** prediction-market volume rose 55% to a record, per Jefferies — [Prediction News](https://predictionnews.com/story/jefferies-prediction-market-volume-jumps-55-to-new-record-in-october)
- **Launch timeline:**
